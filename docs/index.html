Email signatures

Pick your name, verify, copy, paste into Gmail. One button, about a minute.

How to install yours

  1. Find yourself below and open your page.
  2. Press Verify & copy. It checks every image URL first and refuses to copy if any is unreachable.
  3. In Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  4. Paste with Cmd/Ctrl+V, then Save changes at the very bottom of the page.
  5. Send yourself a test and read it on your phone as well as your laptop.
